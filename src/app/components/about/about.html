Our Story

Grown With Care, Baked With Warmth

Matcharap began with one simple idea: bring the calm ritual of whisked matcha together with the comfort of a neighborhood bakery. Every leaf is traceable to the farmers who grow it, and every pastry is shaped by hand before sunrise.

We partner directly with family-run tea gardens in Uji and Nishio, paying above fair-trade rates so the next generation keeps farming matcha the traditional way. Back home, our bakers turn that same care into croissants, mochi donuts, and cakes — using local dairy, seasonal fruit, and zero shortcuts.
